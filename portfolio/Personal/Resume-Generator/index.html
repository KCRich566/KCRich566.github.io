Automatic Resume Generator

Motivation

A resume is often updated in several places: a source document, a portfolio site, and versions tailored to different roles. Repeating the same edits creates drift and makes it easy to forget a project or describe the same experience inconsistently.

The resume generator treats career information as structured source data and produces a document from that source.

Workflow

Career source data
        |
        v
Normalize experience, skills, and projects
        |
        v
Select target role and relevant evidence
        |
        v
Render resume template
        |
        v
Review, export, and publish

Design approach

Experience is stored as reusable records with dates, responsibilities, technologies, and outcomes. A role profile controls ordering and emphasis without duplicating the underlying facts.

The renderer owns presentation: typography, section order, spacing, and output format. The source data owns meaning. Keeping those concerns separate makes template changes safer.

Quality checks

The workflow can validate required fields, date ordering, empty sections, duplicate entries, and link formatting before producing an output. A generated document should still receive a human review, especially when tailored for a specific role.

Why automation helps

Automation reduces mechanical editing and encourages a single source of truth. It does not decide which achievements are meaningful or whether a claim is accurate; those remain deliberate human decisions.

Outcome

The project turns resume maintenance into a repeatable publishing workflow. It supports consistent updates across personal materials while leaving room for role-specific emphasis and final editorial judgment.

Implementation details are described at a product level because the system is a personal tool.
