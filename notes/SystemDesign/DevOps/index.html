DevOps as a Feedback System

DevOps is a way of working that brings development and operations closer around shared outcomes: reliable software, fast feedback, and responsible ownership.

Core loop

Plan a small change, implement it, validate it automatically, deploy it safely, observe its behavior, and use what was learned to improve the next change.

Automation with judgment

Automate repeatable work such as builds, tests, infrastructure changes, and policy checks. Keep human review where context, risk, or customer impact requires it.

Operations as product knowledge

Logs, metrics, traces, alerts, runbooks, and incident reviews turn production behavior into feedback. Teams should measure user impact rather than collect telemetry without a question.
